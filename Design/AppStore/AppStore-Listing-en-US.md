## Description

Retro Recorder: Voice & Text is a Game Boy-inspired recording studio for ideas,
meetings, and everything worth keeping. It combines direct recording controls
with live text, searchable playback, and detailed recording context.

Record your way

- Start recording with one tap.
- Choose built-in, headset, USB, and other audio inputs reported by iOS.
- Select common audio formats, sample rates, bit depth, and channel settings.

See speech as it happens

- Follow live transcription while recording and choose the recognition language.
- Supports Simplified and Traditional Chinese, English, Spanish, Arabic,
  Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, German, and French.
- Re-recognize, edit, compare, copy, and share transcripts after recording.

Find the important parts

- Add time tags while recording or reading Live Text.
- Replay through waveform and transcript views with speed controls, 15-second
  jumps, and output selection.
- Keep the recording time, optional location, audio input, format, sampling,
  encoding, and processing settings with each recording.

Make recordings clearer

- Choose local RNNoise, DeepFilterNet V3, or DTLN noise reduction modes.
- Enable echo cancellation when appropriate for the input route.

Your recordings stay yours

- Recordings are stored locally and can synchronize through your private iCloud
  database across devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
- The app has no advertising or analytics SDKs.
