# Retro Recorder: Voice & Text Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 3, 2026

Retro Recorder: Voice & Text is designed to record audio and organize it with
transcripts, tags, and optional location context. We do not operate an account
system, advertising service, or analytics service for the app.

## Data You Control

Recordings, transcripts, tags, titles, and recording settings are saved on your
device. If iCloud is available for your Apple Account, the app can synchronize
recordings and their associated metadata through your private CloudKit database.
Private CloudKit data belongs to you and is accessible only through your Apple
Account and the app on your devices. The developer cannot browse your private
CloudKit records.

You can delete a recording and its associated local data from the app at any
time. Removing a record also requests removal of its synchronized record.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** Used only when you choose to record audio.
- **Speech Recognition:** Used when you choose real-time or post-recording
  transcription. Recognition is performed through Apple's speech technologies;
  availability and processing behavior can vary by language and device.
- **Location:** Used only after you grant permission, to attach the location
  context that you choose to keep with a recording.
- **iCloud:** Used to synchronize your recordings and metadata between devices
  signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Sharing

The app does not sell data, use advertising identifiers, track you across apps
or websites, or share your recordings with advertising partners. When you use
the system Share action, you choose the destination and control what is shared.
